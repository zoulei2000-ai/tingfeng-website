关于我们About

About关于我们

我们寻找勇往直前、敢于在异国他乡创业的中国年轻人。 We look for young Chinese founders bold enough to build a company far from home.

四十多年来，世界走进中国。接下来的十年，轮到中国的创业者走向世界。

未来是中国的大航海时代。新一代创业者带着在国内练就的产品能力、供应链和运营效率，去美国、中东、东南亚和拉美开公司、建品牌、办工厂，在当地招聘，在当地扎根。

我们相信，10年左右，他们会在海外再造一个中国。听风投资希望从他们出发的第一天起，就站在他们身边。

For more than forty years, the world came to China. In the decade ahead, Chinese founders will go out to the world.

The future is China’s age of sail. A new generation is taking the product skills, supply chains and operating speed they built at home to the United States, the Middle East, Southeast Asia and Latin America. They start companies, build brands and open factories, hire local teams and put down roots.

We believe that in about ten years they will have built another China overseas. Tingfeng wants to be beside them from the day they set out.

我们寻找What we look for

  • 敢出发的人Founders who go

    愿意离开舒适区，在陌生的语言、法律和文化里从零开始，并且坚持下去。People willing to start from zero in an unfamiliar language, legal system and culture, and to stay the course.

  • 中国能力，当地需求China’s strengths, local needs

    把中国的产品、供应链和数字化能力，用来解决当地市场真实存在的问题。Companies that put Chinese products, supply chains and digital know-how to work on real problems in their new market.

  • 扎根当地Built to stay

    组建当地团队，打造当地品牌，做长期的生意，而不是一次性的套利。Teams that hire locally, build local brands and run a long-term business rather than a one-off arbitrage.

    创始合伙人实地调研过66个国家，帮助你更早看清一个市场的机会与陷阱。Our founding partner has researched 66 countries on the ground and can help you see a market’s openings and traps earlier.

A graduate of Shanghai Jiao Tong University, Zou Lei spent many years at Tencent, Huawei, Ping An and Wanda, working across the internet, telecom technology, finance and commercial real estate.

The overseas story began in 1998, with a trip to India to research the market for Huawei. Nearly thirty years of following Chinese brands abroad came after it.

The focus today is the United States and emerging markets such as the Middle East, Indonesia and Latin America. To date, Zou Lei has researched 66 country markets on the ground.
